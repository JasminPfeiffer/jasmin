## Offizielle Beschreibung
Every enterprise AI rollout eventually stops being a technology project and becomes an org design problem. Job descriptions written for execution, teams built around headcount, managers measured on output that agents now produce. This panel goes at the structural rebuild: which roles get redrawn, where the reporting lines break, what upskilling actually costs, and why leadership readiness fails before the tooling does. Enterprise transformation leads and an operator scaling a company through it, on what they had to dismantle before anything worked.
