## Offizielle Beschreibung
The AI Act is not one obligation, it's a chain of them, and everyone in the chain is writing contracts to push them downstream. Then Article 25 reshuffles the deck: rebrand a system, modify it, or change what it's for, and you become the provider. Lawyers and governance leads on where liability actually lands between model providers, integrators and deployers, where the regulation is clear, and where it leaves you carrying risk you never priced.
