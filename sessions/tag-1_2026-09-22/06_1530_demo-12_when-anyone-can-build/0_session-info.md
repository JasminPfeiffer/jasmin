## Offizielle Beschreibung
Building got cheap. Deciding what deserves to be built did not.

At Bricks I shipped production AI agents acting as product builder with coding agents as teammates. I could move faster than ever but I quickly realized that my ability to judge whether what I had just built was actually right dropped. 

This talk is field notes from that gap. What breaks when delivery outruns judgment. Why stuffing more context into agents makes them try to satisfy everything and what I think builders need to change now, whether you’re a PM, an engineer, or a founder watching your team figure out who owns what.
