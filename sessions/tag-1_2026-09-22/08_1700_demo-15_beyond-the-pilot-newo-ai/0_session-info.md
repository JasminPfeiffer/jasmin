## Offizielle Beschreibung
It is easy to build an AI that can hold an impressive conversation. It is much harder to give it responsibility for actual work. Using examples from real deployments across restaurants, dental practices and home services, Ryan McManus breaks down the anatomy of an AI employee: the job it owns, the information it needs, the systems it can access, the decisions it is allowed to make, and the moments when it should hand work back to a human.
