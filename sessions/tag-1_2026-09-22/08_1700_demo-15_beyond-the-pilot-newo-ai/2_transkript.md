## Seite 1

### Demo: Newo.ai

- 95 % AI pilots fail / do not create P&L
  ↳ AI projects are not sticky !
- AI can't replace but it's more filling gaps / people are shifting
- **Why they are not successful?** *(unterstrichen)*
  1) No baseline to compare it to
  2) Pilots are part of innovation dep. but are disconnected from operations
  3) AI do less but better ! – Avoid progressing agents by piling up tasks on them
- **AI employee ⇒ Go beyond pilot** *(unterstrichen)*
  → Give it a clear task // a clear job
  ⇒ Orchestration layer to give **guardrails**
  → write the **job description!**

## Seite 2

AI agent to AI employee ⇒ **guardrails** needed
- Avoid general purpose agents but **give it a small, specific task first**
