## Offizielle Beschreibung
Per-seat pricing assumes headcount. If the product does the work instead of helping a person do it, what exactly is being sold — and to whom?

Tim Beck, Co-Founder and CEO of BLP Digital, on the monetisation question underneath every AI product decision: seats versus outcomes, what inference costs do to gross margin, and which pricing models are quietly breaking.

A commercial conversation, not a product one.
