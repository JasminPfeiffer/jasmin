## Offizielle Beschreibung
What changes when AI stops being something we ask for advice and starts carrying out meaningful parts of a workflow? In this talk, we'll explore the most impactful AI agent workflows we've introduced across our companies, from work automation to data analytics, how they fundamentally changed the way we work, and the many lessons we learned along the way.
