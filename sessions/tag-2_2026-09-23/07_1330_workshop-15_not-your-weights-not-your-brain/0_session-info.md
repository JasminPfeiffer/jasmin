Can open models drive your agentic needs? This session explores the current state of open AI, including local AI. It then goes on to examine how to incorporate open models to your workflow. Open models come in many flavors and sizes: from large near-frontier behemoth models, to small models that can run on your laptop. We'll show when best to use each, and how to integrate them with your favorite tools for conversational and agentic use cases. We'll further explore how quantization libraries such as llama.cpp and MLX allow  large models to run locally, achieving more control, total privacy, low latency, no cloud costs.
